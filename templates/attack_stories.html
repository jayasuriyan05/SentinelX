SentinelX combines related events into an attack sequence using rule-based correlation. A typical simulated attack chain can contain failed login attempts, successful authentication, suspicious PowerShell activity, privileged activity and sensitive file access. As more related stages are detected, the calculated risk increases and the incident can be escalated for investigation.
